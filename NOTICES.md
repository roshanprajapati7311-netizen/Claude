# What Kaivra is made of, and the rights its owner holds

Everything written in this repository is Kaivra's own code and belongs to its owner. The parts below come from others
under licences that let the owner use, change, keep, share and sell them, as long as each licence's notice is kept.
(This is a plain summary, not legal advice.)

| Part | Made by | Licence | What the licence asks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thinking brain (Qwen3.5-2B model, converted and shrunk by Kaivra's own build) | Qwen team, Alibaba Cloud | Apache 2.0 | Keep the licence (shipped as `mind-LICENSE.txt` in the kaivra-packs release) and say that it was changed |
| Engine that runs the thinking brain (llama.cpp and ggml, built into the app) | The ggml authors | MIT | Keep the copyright notice (`app/src/main/assets/licenses/llama.cpp-MIT.txt`) |
| Recorded voices (cut and re-encoded by Kaivra's own builder) | Carnegie Mellon University (CMU ARCTIC) | Free licence, BSD style | Keep the notice (`VOICE-NOTICE.txt` on the voice-pack branch) |
| Pronunciation dictionary | Carnegie Mellon University (CMUdict) | BSD style | Keep the notice |
| Hearing (offline speech recognition) | Alpha Cephei (Vosk) | Apache 2.0 | Keep the licence |
| Knowledge library and the small brain's reading | Simple English Wikipedia writers | CC BY-SA 4.0 | Give credit; share changed copies of the text under the same licence |

Answers that Kaivra finds on the web are read at the moment of asking from public pages (web search results from
DuckDuckGo or Bing, news headlines from Bing News or Google News, and Wikipedia) and are not stored in Kaivra's parts.
Weather comes live from Open-Meteo (https://open-meteo.com/), free and without a key for personal use; its data is
licensed CC BY 4.0, which asks for this credit: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com.
